## QA Checklist

- [ ] The selected category is named and its playbook drives the full setlist.
- [ ] Every named CapCut filter, effect, transition, font, and overlay has a fallback or visual-intent substitute.
- [ ] The first two seconds create curiosity, mood, or movement.
- [ ] The edit is 9:16, at least 15 seconds, and has at least three replaceable slots.
- [ ] The payoff is clear and synchronized to the intended beat or emotional turn.
- [ ] Text is readable inside the vertical safe zone.
- [ ] The template works with portrait, landscape, and low-light replacement media where relevant.
- [ ] No effect stack competes with the category's dominant visual language.
- [ ] No unlicensed audio, copyrighted media, logos, public figures, or watermarks are used.
- [ ] English title, caption, and keywords sound natural and match the current search signal.
