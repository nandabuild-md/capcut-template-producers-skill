# Effects, Transitions, and Fallbacks

This is a compact substitution library. For a complete category recipe, start with style-playbooks.md. Keep one primary filter, one hook effect, and one atmosphere or payoff effect.

## Common Mistakes

- Do not apply a retro texture to a clean unboxing or food template unless retro is the declared category.
- Do not use both Film Flicker and repeated flash cuts; select one impact language.
- Do not use a transition for every clip. Beat-matched hard cuts are usually cleaner.
- Do not use more than two font families or place text outside the vertical safe zone.
- When an exact label is unavailable, match the fallback visual intent rather than selecting a random effect.
