# Effects & Transitions Library

Quick reference for CapCut effects and transitions, organized by use case. Update as you discover new effects.

---

## ⚡ Hook Effects (0–2 seconds)
| Effect | Use case | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| Flash / glitch in | Opening punch, surprise reveal | High |
| Slow zoom in | Emotional opener, memory feel | Low–Medium |
| Split reveal | Before-after, transformation | Medium |
| Film grain fade-in | Nostalgic, vintage aesthetic | Low |
| Beat snap zoom | Sync with first beat hit | High |
