# Style Playbooks — CapCut Production Setlists

Use one recipe as the visual source of truth for a template. CapCut names can vary by region, device, and app release: use the preferred name when present, otherwise use the stated fallback visual intent. Do not combine multiple recipe identities in a single template.

## Shared Setlist Rules

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Filter | Apply one primary filter; make only light manual exposure or temperature adjustments. |
| Effects | Use one hook effect and one atmosphere or payoff effect. Keep effect coverage short and purposeful. |
| Transitions | Cut on beats first. Use a transition only when it expresses a change in place, time, or energy. |
| Typography | Use one headline family and one supporting family. Keep all copy inside the vertical safe zone. |
| Overlays | Use a subtle texture at low opacity; it must support the story rather than hide footage. |
| Labels | Name the preferred CapCut tool, its fallback, the placement, and the intended visual result. |

## 1. Trending-Song / Beat-Sync

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Give users an easy 3–6 slot edit that lands every cut on a recognizable beat or lyric cue. |
| Filter | Primary: Vivid. Fallback: Fresh or a clean high-contrast look. |
| Effects | Hook: Beat Snap Zoom for 0:00–0:00.5. Payoff: Flash Cut on the first chorus hit. |
| Transitions | Mostly hard cuts; use Swipe Left or Vertical Blur II only for a directional beat change. |
| Typography | Headline: Kanit Bold. Support: Sora Bold. Use 2–4 word lyric-neutral hooks. |
| Overlay | Minimal grain or no overlay; retain crisp subject separation. |
| Rhythm and audio | 0.4–0.9 second cuts, matched to beat markers; add Bass Hit or Camera Click only at payoff. |
| Avoid | Long crossfades, off-beat cuts, copied lyrics, or more than one impact effect. |

## 2. Daily Vlog

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Make ordinary routines feel warm, effortless, and easy for users to replace. |
| Filter | Primary: Sunkissed. Fallback: Dolce or a warm low-contrast grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Slow Zoom In. Atmosphere: Film Frame or Noise 2 for short clips only. |
| Transitions | Dissolve, Light Sweep, or Pull In between location changes; use simple cuts within a scene. |
| Typography | Headline: Sofia Pro. Support: Lucette. Use sentence-case diary copy. |
| Overlay | Fine Film Grain at low opacity plus a restrained date stamp. |
| Rhythm and audio | 1–2 second clips with room for breathing; lo-fi or soft indie at 70–95 BPM, light Whoosh accents. |
| Avoid | Heavy glitch, harsh saturation, tiny text, or a transition between every clip. |

## 3. Travel

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Turn a trip into a forward-moving mini escape with a clear sense of place. |
| Filter | Primary: Summer V. Fallback: Jazz or a bright teal-warm travel grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Split Reveal from map/detail to wide shot. Payoff: Light Leak or Lens Flare at golden-hour footage. |
| Transitions | Whip Pan, Swipe Left, and Zoom Blur only when motion direction matches. |
| Typography | Headline: Sora Bold. Support: Work Sans Black. Use location or itinerary fragments. |
| Overlay | Subtle film grain, map-coordinate text, or dust particles under 20% opacity. |
| Rhythm and audio | Sequence detail, movement, wide view, human moment, final vista; 100–120 BPM indie/electronic with Wind Ambient. |
| Avoid | Random city changes without a bridge, fake location claims, or inconsistent blue/orange grades. |

## 4. Birthday

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Deliver a festive reveal template that works for a single portrait or a group memory dump. |
| Filter | Primary: Dolce. Fallback: Vivid or a warm bright celebratory grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Flash In with the first portrait. Payoff: Glowburst II on cake, candles, or final frame. |
| Transitions | Light Sweep, Mix, and short Flash Cut on upbeat sections. |
| Typography | Headline: Kanit Bold. Support: Handwriting. Keep the name and age optional. |
| Overlay | Confetti, bokeh, or small sparkle overlay at low-to-medium density. |
| Rhythm and audio | 0.7–1.2 second cuts; celebratory pop at 100–125 BPM with Applause or Chime Ting at the reveal. |
| Avoid | Overlapping celebration overlays, unreadable cursive, or personal details beyond user-supplied copy. |

## 5. Anniversary / Couple Memories

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Create a soft emotional memory sequence with a single intimate ending. |
| Filter | Primary: Sepia. Fallback: Sunkissed or a warm faded film grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Film Grain Fade-In. Payoff: Bokeh Overlay with a gentle Slow Zoom. |
| Transitions | Dissolve, Black Fade, and Film Overlay; leave short pauses before the ending. |
| Typography | Headline: Lucette. Support: Sofia Pro. Use a short English sentiment, not a quotation. |
| Overlay | Soft bokeh, dust, and date text at low opacity. |
| Rhythm and audio | 1.2–2 second clips; 70–85 BPM piano, ambient pop, or acoustic with a soft Chime Ting. |
| Avoid | Aggressive motion blur, stock romance phrases, or high-energy effects. |

## 6. Religion / Reflection

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Support respectful reflection, gratitude, or a quiet seasonal observance without sensationalism. |
| Filter | Primary: Noon. Fallback: Film or a neutral warm low-saturation grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Fade In. Atmosphere: Dust Particles or a very light Film Frame. |
| Transitions | Crossfade, Black Fade, and slow Push In; edits should feel intentional and calm. |
| Typography | Headline: Rockwell. Support: Trebuchet. Use short, respectful original text. |
| Overlay | Soft light rays or restrained particles under 15% opacity. |
| Rhythm and audio | 1.5–2.5 second clips; ambient, piano, or acoustic instrumentals with no disruptive SFX. |
| Avoid | Comic effects, strobe transitions, claims of authority, attacks on beliefs, or sacred imagery used as decoration. |

## 7. Fitness / Gym / Sport

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Make a training sequence feel progressive and energetic while preserving a clear before-to-after payoff. |
| Filter | Primary: Vivid. Fallback: Fresh or a high-clarity contrast grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Beat Snap Zoom. Payoff: Shake or Vibration for one rep, finish, or score moment. |
| Transitions | Whip Pan, Motion Blur Swipe, and Flash Cut on impact beats. |
| Typography | Headline: Kanit Bold. Support: Source Code. Use compact labels such as DAY ONE or KEEP GOING. |
| Overlay | Minimal speed lines, timer, or progress bar; keep the subject unobstructed. |
| Rhythm and audio | 0.4–0.8 second cuts; 120–140 BPM electronic or hip-hop instrumental with Bass Hit and Whoosh. |
| Avoid | Excessive shake, misleading health claims, unsafe exercise demonstrations, or unreadable timer overlays. |

## 8. Fashion / Cutout / GRWM

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Create a confident editorial reveal with clean silhouette, fabric detail, and outfit payoff. |
| Filter | Primary: Jazz. Fallback: Humble or a polished neutral-contrast grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Split Reveal. Payoff: Film Flicker or Flash Cut on final look. |
| Transitions | Scribble for playful cutout moments, Film Flicker for editorial changes, and Swipe Left for outfit swaps. |
| Typography | Headline: Sora Bold. Support: Sofia Pro. Use all caps sparingly for OOTD or GRWM. |
| Overlay | Clean frame lines, low-opacity grain, or small editorial timestamp. |
| Rhythm and audio | 0.5–1 second cuts; 90–115 BPM R&B or confident chill-trap, Camera Click at outfit reveal. |
| Avoid | Multiple font families, body-altering claims, strong VHS unless the concept is deliberately retro, or cluttered cutouts. |

## 9. Business / Unboxing

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Present an object, workspace, or process with clarity and a satisfying reveal, without turning it into an unverified sales claim. |
| Filter | Primary: Fresh. Fallback: Vivid or a bright neutral product grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Slow Zoom In on a detail. Payoff: Light Sweep across the final setup or reveal. |
| Transitions | Pull In, Swipe Left, and clean Match Cut between box, detail, and use. |
| Typography | Headline: Work Sans Black. Support: Sora Bold. Use concise descriptive labels. |
| Overlay | Minimal callout line, spec card, or clean frame; use no decorative texture by default. |
| Rhythm and audio | 0.8–1.4 second clips; 90–110 BPM minimal electronic with soft Click or Whoosh. |
| Avoid | Price promises, unsupported product claims, logos without permission, or effects that obscure the product. |

## 10. Kids / Baby / Family

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Preserve playful family milestones with a gentle, privacy-aware memory-film feel. |
| Filter | Primary: Dolce. Fallback: Noon or a soft warm pastel grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Gentle Slow Zoom. Atmosphere: Film Frame or Bokeh for a short final moment. |
| Transitions | Dissolve, Light Sweep, and soft Pull In. |
| Typography | Headline: Handwriting. Support: Sofia Pro. Keep captions simple and optional. |
| Overlay | Small stars, doodles, or bokeh at low density. |
| Rhythm and audio | 1–1.8 second clips; 75–105 BPM light acoustic or playful instrumental with soft Chime Ting. |
| Avoid | Names, locations, schools, schedules, embarrassing footage, invasive close-ups, or loud strobe effects. |

## 11. Motivation / Glow-Up

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Build from a reflective opening to one confident, repeatable transformation payoff. |
| Filter | Primary: Film for the opening, then Vivid at payoff. Fallback: Humble opening, Fresh payoff. |
| Effects | Hook: Film Grain Fade-In. Payoff: Flash Cut plus one Beat Snap Zoom. |
| Transitions | Black Fade before change, then Flash Cut or Vertical Blur II after it. |
| Typography | Headline: Source Code or Kanit Bold. Support: Sora Bold. Use original, direct micro-copy. |
| Overlay | Progress line or subtle before/after label; remove it at final reveal. |
| Rhythm and audio | Start 70–90 BPM then resolve at a beat drop; use Heartbeat before payoff and Bass Hit at reveal. |
| Avoid | Empty inspirational clichés, false achievement claims, overlong quote blocks, or more than one transformation moment. |

## 12. Food / Café / Restaurant Visit

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Make texture, steam, pour, and place feel appetizing while keeping the edit quick and usable. |
| Filter | Primary: Sunkissed. Fallback: Jazz or a warm rich food grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Slow Zoom In on texture. Payoff: Light Leak or Glowburst II on pour, bite, or table-wide reveal. |
| Transitions | Match Cut, Pull In, and short Light Sweep between food details. |
| Typography | Headline: Rockwell. Support: Lucette. Use dish, mood, or location-neutral copy. |
| Overlay | Low-opacity grain, menu-like rule, or subtle steam; do not cover food. |
| Rhythm and audio | Detail, preparation, wide table, reaction, final hero shot; 85–110 BPM indie/chill beat with Camera Click or Chime Ting. |
| Avoid | Extreme color saturation, hygiene claims, logos without permission, or every-shot transitions. |

## 13. Trailer / Teaser / Cinematic

| Setlist element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Goal | Build a short cinematic arc: mystery, detail, escalation, then a decisive title-card or final vista. |
| Filter | Primary: Film. Fallback: Modern or a moody low-saturation cinematic grade. |
| Effects | Hook: Fade In from black with Film Frame. Payoff: Zoom Blur or Flash Cut followed by a held final frame. |
| Transitions | Black Fade for chapters, Zoom Blur for momentum, and Film Flicker only at an intentional cut point. |
| Typography | Headline: Rockwell or Sora Bold. Support: Trebuchet. Use 2–6 word title cards. |
| Overlay | Film Grain, Vignette, Dust Particles, or a light leak; use one texture family only. |
| Rhythm and audio | Begin with 1.5–2.5 second shots, compress toward 0.4–0.8 seconds at the build, then hold 1–2 seconds on payoff; 60–90 BPM cinematic/ambient with Deep Boom and Whoosh. |
| Avoid | Unmotivated glitch, dense title cards, trailer claims that imply a real release, or stacking grain, VHS, flare, and dust together. |
